Virtual folders

    
Extra folders at the top of the mount, each listing the jobs that match all its filters (a name regex and a files regex together match either). Useful in Plex mode: point a Movies library at one folder and a TV library at another, e.g. shows with regex (?i)S\d{2}(E\d{2})?|Season and movies with not_regex the same. Sizes take 10GB / 500MB, last_added takes 7d / 12h, protocol takes usenet or debrid.
