NFO Files

From 3 December 2026 Plex numbers TV shows by TMDB, while Clyde names your episode files by its own metadata (TVDB numbering, or the episode order you picked for a show). Where the two disagree, Plex shows the wrong episode titles. NFO files next to your episodes carry Clyde's title, plot and air date for exactly the episode in each file. Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and Kodi all read them.

NFO files need Symlinked/Local file management: in Plex mode Clyde has no library folder to write them to.
Save Plex's current episode details

Reads what Plex shows today for every episode in the TV library (TheTVDB titles, descriptions and air dates, and anything you edited by hand) and saves it as NFO files next to the episodes. After 3 December Plex can only refill episodes from TMDB, so take it before then. Plex isn't changed, and this works with NFO files Off: the files just wait until you switch Plex (or Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi) to an agent that reads them. NFO files Clyde makes for a mode below, and NFO files you made yourself, are never replaced. Upgraded episodes keep theirs. Every snapshot is also kept in plex_metadata_snapshot.db.

It's 3 December or later: Plex may already show TMDB's details for refreshed shows, so a new snapshot saves those. Older snapshots below are kept.
NFO files for my TV library
  • Files go under : tvshow.nfo (ids only) and one .nfo per episode file, kept up to date as episodes are added, upgraded, removed or renumbered.
  • With Plex, turning NFO files on switches the TV library to an agent that reads them (then refreshes it); shows without NFO files keep matching through Plex Series exactly as before. Off puts Plex's previous agent back and deletes the NFO files Clyde wrote (snapshot NFOs come back in their place). NFO files you made yourself are never changed or deleted.

Plex

The agent Clyde makes, Clyde NFO + Plex Series, reads the NFO file first, then Plex Series for anything the NFO doesn't say (cast, artwork, Discover), then local media. Shows keep their Plex identity, so watch history and collections stay. Needs Plex Media Server 1.43.1 or newer.
