Stale metadata. These items have battery metadata that hasn't been refreshed within the expected window. Returning/active shows refresh every 6 hours, ended shows every 30 days, and movies every 90 days. Stale data can cause incorrect episode schedules or upgrade decisions. Click Refresh to update them.

Orphaned TVDB→IMDB mappings. The battery keeps a lookup table that translates TVDB IDs (used by the TVDB metadata source) into IMDB IDs. These entries point to IMDB IDs that no longer exist in the battery — meaning the show they referenced has been removed. Deleting them is safe; the mapping will be automatically recreated the next time that show is looked up.

Orphaned TMDB→IMDB mappings. The battery keeps a lookup table that translates TMDB IDs (used for poster fetching and metadata supplementation) into IMDB IDs. These entries point to IMDB IDs that no longer exist in the battery — meaning the movie or show they referenced has been removed. Deleting them is safe; the mapping will be automatically recreated the next time that title is looked up.
