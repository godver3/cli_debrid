Export / Import

Share your setup as a file, or start from someone else's. API keys, passwords, tokens, URLs, paths, servers and accounts never go in the file.

Export

Pick what to include, then download. The file is safe to post: it holds no keys, logins or addresses.

Import

Choose a settings file. You'll see every change before anything is saved. Your keys, logins, paths and servers are never touched, and nothing is removed. New scrapers and lists arrive turned off.

Your current settings are saved to settings_backups/ in your config folder first.
