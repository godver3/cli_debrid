Library Manager

Automatically add deleted shows and movies to the ghostlist to prevent them from being re-added. When enabled, deleting a show will mark it as ghosted in the database. When disabled, deleted shows can be re-added by content sources.

Remove items from content sources (Trakt, Overseerr, etc.) during deletion. Enable when ghostlist OFF (prevents re-addition, slower). Disable when ghostlist ON (faster, ghostlist already prevents re-addition).

Hide Season 0 (Specials) tab and its episodes from the TV show page.

Remove all cached posters and backdrops. Artwork will be re-downloaded from the primary source on next library load.
