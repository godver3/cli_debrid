AI Assistant (MCP)

Connect Claude (Desktop or Code) or another MCP-capable AI assistant to cli_debrid, so it can explain your settings, read your logs and help troubleshoot. It is read-only: the assistant can look, never change anything. API keys, tokens and passwords are masked in everything it sees.

Anyone with the token below can read your settings (secrets masked), logs and library. Keep it private; make a new one if it leaks.
